Review date: 2026-10-21  
Evidence update: 2026-10-23  
Fixtures: Radhika Physiotherapy reference; calm, local-access, and process-led shortlist briefs; all 20 explicit candidate directions  
Status: local industry-pack certification passed; customer identity and remote release acceptance remain separate

## Decision

The audited staging catalog became the source evidence for the certified `physiotherapy-flagship-2026.10.23` pack. All 20 candidate identities are now registered through a separate certified runtime with pack-owned ranking, scaffold, selection, structure/content/media, logo, and publish validation. The old draft route remains archived regression evidence, and none of these internal fixtures constitutes approval of a real clinic's logo or business facts.

The design no longer resembles the Dental system. It uses a movement-pathway grammar: an asymmetric organic hero image, a quiet movement arc, staggered service cards, a connected assessment path, and a warm kinetic CTA motif. These rules are scoped to `data-mi-subtype="physiotherapy"` and do not restyle Dental.

## What changed after the failed visual review

- Replaced the condensed, all-caps, ultra-heavy H1 with a readable sentence-case proposition.
- Bounded mobile H1 size and leading; the reference mobile result is 32 px with 1.1 line height.
- Fixed the 320 px header collision by constraining the brand track and allowing a safe two-line business name.
- Replaced the one-image result with seven meaningful Home images and a 12-image site pool.
- Reserved every page hero asset so it cannot be reused in supporting cards.
- Removed repeated appointment forms from Home and About; Contact owns the only full form.
- Rewrote the empty Therapists state as verification guidance instead of inventing clinicians.
- Added physiotherapy-only arcs, staggered cards, a connected process line, and a kinetic CTA treatment.
- Reduced excessive desktop height, converted five-card service groups to a balanced wide-screen rhythm, and centered concise information bands.
- Grouped every CTA with its heading and supporting copy instead of leaving the action stranded in a separate column.
- Replaced internal compliance language such as “supplied,” “verified,” and “invented proof” with natural patient-facing guidance while retaining the same internal safety gates.
- Expanded the no-profile Therapists state into three useful questions and removed semantically weak supporting stock images from Therapists and Contact.
